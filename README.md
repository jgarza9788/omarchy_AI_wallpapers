# Omarchy AI Wallpapers

![25 Omarchy wallpaper designs](docs/hero.jpg)

**25 designs, 160+ wallpapers at 3840×2160** for [Omarchy](https://omarchy.us), made with
Blender, GIMP, ImageMagick, numpy and Nerd Fonts. Every color variant uses a real Omarchy theme's `colors.toml`,
so it matches the theme you actually run, and many designs come in the official brand colors (`--scheme omarchy`).
Everything is generated by the scripts in `src/` and can be rebuilt with `./build_all.sh`.

Made in a single long session with Claude Code (Anthropic), directed by [@jgarza9788](https://github.com/jgarza9788).

## The designs

### Round 1 (01–05)
| # | Wallpaper | Style | Tools | Schemes |
|---|-----------|-------|-------|---------|
| 01 | **Monolith** | 3D render: the OMA mark (circle · notched square · diamond) rebuilt as 3D geometry, sliced into its 5 color bands as staggered slabs on a glossy floor, with neon halo rings | Blender 5.2 (bpy, Cycles) | tokyo-night, gruvbox, ethereal, osaka-jade, matte-black |
| 02 | **Topography** | Generative contour map: the OMARCHY wordmark rises as a flat-topped mesa, with lines colored by elevation and map labels | Python/numpy, ImageMagick, Iosevka NF | everforest, nord, kanagawa, catppuccin, flexoki-light |
| 03 | **Glyph Rain** | Terminal "matrix" rain of Nerd Font dev icons in 3 depth layers around the ASCII Omarchy logo, with a neon glow | numpy glyph atlas, ImageMagick, **GIMP 3** (Python-Fu glow), Iosevka NF | hackerman, tokyo-night, retro-82, miasma, ristretto |
| 04 | **Pixel Dusk** | 480×270 pixel art: the OMA mark as a striped synthwave sun over ridges and a lake, dithered to *only* the theme's colors, upscaled 8× | numpy, **GIMP 3** (indexed conversion with a custom palette, positioned dither, nearest-neighbour scale) | gruvbox, catppuccin, everforest, ethereal, last-horizon |
| 05 | **Bauhaus Grid** | Flat geometric tiles in the logo's shape vocabulary, denser toward the bottom-right so the top-left stays calm; recolored OMA mark | Python → SVG → rsvg-convert, Space Mono NF | rose-pine (dawn), nord, lumon, solitude, white |

### Round 2: outside the box (06–10)
The first five were judged too derivative, so the second round starts from a *process,
data source or format* instead of a look. In every one of these the variants change
**form**, not just color.

| # | Wallpaper | Idea | Tools | Variants |
|---|-----------|------|-------|----------|
| 06 | **Suminagashi** | Exact *mathematical marbling* (Jaffer & Lu): ink drops and tine/wave/swirl combs as closed-form deformations; each pixel is traced backward through the recipe. The theme name seeds the recipe, so every scheme is a different marbling | numpy | kanagawa, rose-pine, osaka-jade, catppuccin-latte, miasma |
| 07 | **Your System, as a Galaxy** | The real `pacman` dependency graph of this machine (1,066 packages, 5,388 dependencies on the author's machine). Dependency depth sets the radius (glibc in the bulge), a force layout sets the angle, and a density wave makes spiral arms. Each scheme lights up one app's dependency constellation | numpy, pacman (rebuilds from *your* installed packages) | tokyo-night → hyprland, catppuccin → blender, gruvbox → gimp, ethereal → omarchy, nord → libreoffice |
| 08 | **Shroud** | Blender cloth simulation: silk dropped over hidden forms (the OMA circle, square and diamond, never shown). Each scheme gets its own sheet rotation and wind gust, but the drapes come out very similar; the variants differ mainly in silk color and studio lighting | Blender (cloth sim + Cycles) | rose-pine, kanagawa, everforest, retro-82, vantablack |
| 09 | **Sigils** | Each theme's name is hashed into Sprott-style 12-letter codes for a quadratic map, searched until the attractor is chaotic, so every theme grows its own one-off shape. Code and Lyapunov exponent printed as a signature | numpy | vantablack, lumon, ristretto, hackerman, white |
| 10 | **Sundial** | A wallpaper made of time: 24 hourly frames with shadows from the real sun position (your latitude, today's declination). The dial's hour lines are where the gnomon's shadow falls. The sun's elevation picks the theme, and after sunset the gnomon becomes a lamp casting radial shadows | numpy | 24 frames: white → flexoki-light → rose-pine → twilight → ethereal → tokyo-night |

### App Galaxy remixes (13–17)
These were preceded by research into what's popular. Wallhaven's 1-year toplists favor: huge negative space
around a small subject, thin glowing lines on black, single-hue motion/speed smears, sculpted
monochrome ribbons with prismatic edges, hyperspace starbursts, and images built from glyphs.
Each remix runs a different physical process on App Galaxy's icons (shared code: `src/appfield.py`).

| # | Wallpaper | The physics | Variants |
|---|-----------|-------------|----------|
| 13 | **Event Horizon** | An icon accretion disk around a black hole, lensed by the point-lens equation θ± = (b ± √(b²+4θE²))/2: the far side arcs over the shadow, a secondary ring appears below, icons stretch along the arcs, the inner orbit spaghettifies, and the approaching side is Doppler-bright | matte-black, tokyo-night, vantablack, catppuccin |
| 14 | **Collapse** | A launcher grid with a new mass (the Omarchy mark). Icons break loose sooner the closer they are (t ∝ d^2.2) and fall on integrated Newtonian orbits as glowing motion smears, while far icons only lean | ristretto, nord, hackerman, gruvbox |
| 15 | **Wake** | A rocket crosses the grid: a widening wake plus a Kármán vortex street of alternating vortices swirl and tumble icons out of their slots, with trails home. The grid ahead is untouched | retro-82, catppuccin, kanagawa, miasma |
| 16 | **Merger** | A Toomre & Toomre restricted N-body sim: a "system" galaxy and an "apps" galaxy on a parabolic encounter, leapfrog-integrated past pericentre until tidal tails and a bridge swap their icons | tokyo-night, ethereal, everforest, kanagawa |
| 17 | **Lightspeed** | Jumping through a 3D lattice of app grids: every icon streaks radially (∝ speed/depth²) out of a dark tunnel toward the Omarchy mark at the vanishing point | hackerman, tokyo-night, retro-82, lumon |

Inspiration sources: [Wallhaven toplist](https://wallhaven.cc/toplist) (1-year, general and abstract),
[Wallhaven API](https://wallhaven.cc/help/api), [wallpapers.com trend roundup](https://wallpapers.com/blog/most-popular-desktop-wallpapers/).

### 23 · OMA Blend (from `assets/OMA.blend`)
Built from the hand-modelled OMA in `assets/OMA.blend`. `oma_blend.py` appends the O (Cylinder), M (Cube.002, notched by
a Boolean with Cube.003) and A (Cube.001), removes their Array modifiers, applies the Boolean, and keeps the O-M-A
column order and orientation exactly. Icons are real Blender text objects set in Iosevka Nerd Font with emissive
materials, so the glass refracts and the metal reflects actual icons.

| Render | Scene |
|--------|-------|
| `oma-icon-wall` | a single glass OMA column from `assets/OMA.blend`, using that file's camera, in front of a wall of glowing icons in the OMA greens (`src/23-oma-blend/oma_icon_wall.py`) |
| `oma-grid` | 3 rows × 9 OMA columns rotating 0°→180° left to right; rows are crystal / chrome / iridescent glass, in front of a glowing icon wall (`src/23-oma-blend/oma_blend.py`; that script also has untested `hero` and `wave` layouts) |

### The official Omarchy palette (`--scheme omarchy`)
omarchy.us/brand publishes logos only, with no color spec. The de facto official scheme is the default theme,
**Tokyo Night** (set by `install/user/theme.sh`), with the brand green **`#9ece6a`** as the accent; every official
logo is filled with it. The OMA logo's five bands `#daecc6 #bbdd97 #9ece6a #678549 #39482e` are stored too, and
23 uses them for its icon glow. `palettes/palettes.py` adds this as a palette named `omarchy`, rendered for
11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 22 and 23.

### 24 · OMA Dome (variations on `assets/OMA_00.blend`)
A hand-built scene: a 3-row O / M / A grid, arrayed into columns that turn from left to right, in front of a huge
mirror sphere. The camera has depth of field focused on the first O at f/0.1. The script keeps all of that
untouched and only re-lights it for Cycles, so the glass truly refracts: the sphere's EEVEE Specular shader
renders black in Cycles, and the glass rendered dark grey.

| Variant | Look |
|---------|------|
| `greenhouse` | the dome glows in Omarchy greens, brightest behind the grid, fading to dark on the right |
| `icon-dome` | the dome is tiled with glowing Nerd Font icons (`src/24-oma-dome/icons.png`); the shallow focus turns them into green bokeh |
| `mirror` | the original green mirror sphere ported to Cycles, reflecting a green-to-black sky like a horizon |
| `long-shadow` | a matte white dome and one low, hard green spot: long crisp shadows rake across the wall |

`blender -b assets/OMA_00.blend -P src/24-oma-dome/oma_dome.py -- --variant all`

### 25 · Twist (variations on `assets/OMA_01.blend`)
A hand-built twisted wordmark: the Omarchy SVG given thickness with Solidify, twisted about 126° along its length with SimpleDeform,
smoothed and tilted. The wordmark is built from pixel strips, so the twist fans each letter into spiralling
slats. The object, its modifiers and the camera position are kept; the depth-of-field focus moves from the
hidden O onto the word.

| Variant | Look |
|---------|------|
| `glass` | crystal wordmark with green rim lights along the slats, in front of a soft green glow |
| `neon` | the slats glow from deep logo green to brand green along the word, over a dark glossy floor |
| `shadows` | matte white wordmark, one low hard sun light: a long twisted striped shadow across a pale wall |

`blender -b assets/OMA_01.blend -P src/25-twist/twist.py -- --variant all`

**Sundial live:** `src/10-sundial/sundial-tick.sh` sets the frame for the current hour. To run it hourly:
```bash
mkdir -p ~/.config/systemd/user
cp src/10-sundial/sundial.{service,timer} ~/.config/systemd/user/
systemctl --user daemon-reload && systemctl --user enable --now sundial.timer
```
(Note: `install.sh` skips sundial frames because they're keyed by hour, not theme.)

## Layout
```
assets/*.blend     hand-built Blender scenes used by 23–25
docs/              README hero + thumbnails (make_readme_previews.sh)
.claude/skills/    a Claude Code skill describing how to add wallpapers here
assets/brand/      official Omarchy marks (wordmark, logo, oma-logo; svg + 4096px png)
assets/fonts/      Nerd Fonts: Iosevka, CaskaydiaCove, SpaceMono, 3270 (Mono weights only;
                   also installed to ~/.local/share/fonts/wp-nerdfonts)
palettes/          palettes.py extracts /usr/share/omarchy/themes/*/colors.toml -> palettes.json
src/common.py      shared numpy/ImageMagick helpers
src/oma_shapes.py  exact O / M / A outlines from the OMA logo (used by 12)
src/appfield.py    icon atlas, glyph warps/trails, camera, splatting, branding (11, 13–17)
src/NN-name/       one generator per wallpaper (+ GIMP batch scripts for 03/04, systemd units for 10)
output/NN-name/    the wallpapers: <name>--<scheme>.png (05 also keeps its .svg)
output/_previews/  a contact sheet per wallpaper + gallery.png
```

## Rebuild
```bash
./build_all.sh            # everything (the three Blender pieces take several hours on CPU)
./build_all.sh --fast     # skip the Blender pieces
./make_readme_previews.sh # refresh docs/hero.jpg and docs/thumbs/
python3 src/02-topography/topography.py --scheme nord --scale 0.25   # quick preview -> .wip/
blender -b -P src/01-monolith/monolith.py -- --scheme gruvbox --samples 24 --scale 25
```
Every generator takes `--scheme <omarchy-theme>` (any installed theme works, not just
the five listed) or `all`. Randomness is seeded, so builds are reproducible.

## Use them
```bash
./install.sh   # copies each wallpaper to ~/.config/omarchy/backgrounds/<its theme>/
```
Then switch to that theme and cycle backgrounds (`omarchy-theme-bg-next`). You can also open
any of them in **OmaPhoto** for hand touch-ups. It's GUI-only, so it isn't part of the scripted build.

## Requirements
Arch / Omarchy with `blender` (5.x), `gimp` (3.x), `imagemagick` (7, with rsvg), `python` + `numpy`. Color schemes are
read from `/usr/share/omarchy/themes/*/colors.toml`, so the generators expect an Omarchy install. The Nerd Fonts used
are vendored in `assets/fonts/`.
